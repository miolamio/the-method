# A shared score for movement and sound, sight-readable by dancers and musicians

> No ready Method found: this is a way to understand the task, not a proven Method for a class · Heuristic

## Task

A composer plans five years of evenings and summers on one score that dancers and musicians can both sight-read.

- **Unknown:** the notation and its stages; whether it is research, product or art is settled in S7.
- **Given:** music-notation expertise, two choreographers, dance notations that are slow to learn and separate from music.
- **Success condition:** a dancer and a musician perform a short passage together from the page on first reading.

## Problem Class

- **Class:** None found
- **Candidates considered:**
  - Iterative user-centred design of a notation - rejected: steps confirmed (R1), notation evaluation confirmed (R2); use on dance or combined notation not found.
  - Design-based research - rejected: confirmed only for educational representations (R5).
  - Practice-based artistic research - rejected: one source, music notation only (R8).
  - Product development - rejected: the goal names no buyer or market.
- **Parts with recognisable classes:** prior-art survey is a literature review (S3); reading tests of prototypes are usability evaluation of a notation (S5); making pieces with the score is practice-based artistic research (S6); choosing the project's framing is a personal decision (S7).

- **Heuristic:** readings vary between readers, and feedback comes only at rehearsals. The Plan therefore uses small trials, each with a written prediction that is checked afterwards (S4-S6).

## Assumptions

- **A1.** "Sight-read" means performing a short passage after one silent read-through, not a full work. If wrong: the success condition, S1 and S5 change.
- **A2.** Both choreographers can give a few sessions a year. If wrong: S4-S6 use dancers you recruit, such as students at your school.
- **A3.** The aim is a notation used in practice, not a degree or a commercial product yet. If wrong: the framing chosen in S7 comes first.
- **A4.** Paper and general drawing software are enough for prototypes in year one. If wrong: add a software part in S2.
- **A5.** You can find dancers and musicians who have not seen the material, to read it cold. If wrong: S5 uses the collaborators, a weaker proxy.
- **A6.** The repertoire is set choreography with composed music, not improvisation. If wrong: what the score must encode changes; revisit S1.
- **A7.** No funding or institution is needed to start. If wrong: the framing decision in S7 moves before S4.

- **Success check:** You and one choreographer judge it. The signal is that in S5 or S6 a dancer and a musician performed a passage together from the page, on first reading, with fewer stops than when they worked from video and notes. Every trial has its prediction and result written down.

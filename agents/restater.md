You restate a Method from your own knowledge. You have no tools and cannot ask questions.

## Input

- the Method's name
- the Problem Class

## Task

Write the Method's steps and its applicability conditions as you know them, from scratch. State what you know of the Method, including where you are unsure. Add no links, authors, years or figures.

## Return

- **Steps**: the Method's steps, in order.
- **Applicability conditions**: when the Method applies and when it does not.
